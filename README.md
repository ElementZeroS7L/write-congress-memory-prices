# Write Congress: AI Is Pricing Us Out of Our Own Hardware

A free, single-page tool that helps people write their U.S. representative and senators about AI-driven memory (DRAM) and graphics card prices.

- Find your representative and senators
- Add your own story (offices weigh personal letters far more than form copies)
- Copy the letter and paste it into your official's contact form

**No ads, no analytics, no cookies, no data collected.** Everything you type stays in your browser. The whole site is one file, `index.html`, so anyone can read it, fork it or improve it.

Every claim in the letter is sourced on the page itself.
